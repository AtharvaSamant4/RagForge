# Acme customer handbook

## Refund policy

Acme offers a 30 day refund period for all annual subscriptions. To request a refund, email support@acme.example with your invoice number. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within seven business days.

## Support hours

Acme support is available Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 UTC. Customers on the Enterprise plan receive a response within four business hours.

## Data retention

Acme retains uploaded customer documents until the customer deletes them. Deleting a document removes its retrieval index entries. Backups expire after thirty days.

## Integrations

Acme supports REST API integration using project-scoped API credentials. Store private credentials on your application server and revoke unused keys.
